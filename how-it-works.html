How Temper Lab works

How to use it

  • Once a month, after the month's last close, open Research. The Shortlist is the review queue: companies on two or more lists, with positive shareholders' equity. Read each one's Ticker page, decide, and plan to hold 6–12 months.
  • Between reviews, do not act on weekly reshuffles. A name entering or leaving the Shortlist's first ten for a week or two is usually noise: in a 2022–26 replay, half of all stays lasted two weeks or less.
  • Every week, scan for news that changes a view: Insights (insider and 5%+ holder filings), Jev reads (results), next results dates, Market mood.
  • The trend colour changes once a month. In our test a change between month-ends did not predict returns; the month-end state did.

How the data is built

  • Universe: US-listed common stocks. A company counts as liquid with a market value of at least $1B, average daily trading of at least $10M over 63 sessions and a price of at least $5.
  • Prices: Yahoo daily closes, adjusted for splits and dividends.
  • Company numbers: SEC filings (XBRL company facts), each used only from the day it was filed — point in time, never back-dated. Twelve-month figures add up four quarters.
  • If a week's SEC download is incomplete, the companies it missed keep the filings saved earlier, and the Research page says so.
  • Limits: the price history holds companies that are listed today, so past tests miss companies that disappeared. Results are before costs.

What the tests found

  • Trend rules (price above its 200-day average; 12-month return, skipping the last month, above zero; 50-day above 200-day): stocks with each rule up beat those with it down by 3.0–3.6% over six months (entries 2021–26, stocks of similar size compared, before costs).
  • Short interest: lightly shorted stocks (days to cover) led, and heavily shorted ones trailed, stocks of similar size over six months (2023–26).
  • Profitability: Growth ranks on five measures (return on equity, return on assets, and operating income, cash flow and gross profit to assets), not on return on equity alone, which rewards thin equity. On 2021–26 data it ranked as well, with less leverage.
  • F-Score did not pass, so it is shown as a checklist only.
  • Forward tests: each Saturday's first ten on Growth and the Shortlist are recorded and checked against QQQ after 6 and 12 months.

Past results, not a forecast, and not a buy or sell call.
