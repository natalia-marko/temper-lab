      How to read these numbers, and their limits

      Who is here. Every company on this week’s Hot tape, Growth or Cheap list, with the Shortlist marked. A company on several lists shows each list’s rank. The default order is the list’s own rank, not anything analysts say.

      Ratings mix and Buy share. Yahoo’s five labels: Strong Buy, Buy, Hold, Sell, Strong Sell. Hold is Yahoo’s label, not “keep”. Buy share = (Strong Buy + Buy) ÷ all five. With fewer than three ratings one analyst moves it a lot, so those names are hidden by default. Mix vs −1m is this month’s Buy share minus last month’s, in percentage points, shown when both months have 3+ ratings; coverage can change between months (n: last → now), so it is a change in mix, not a count of upgrades. A missing summary is shown as n/a, never counted as zero votes.

      EPS 30d. Upward and downward revisions to the current fiscal year’s EPS estimate over the last 30 days, and how far the average estimate moved against 90 days ago.

      Targets and implied upside. Median target against the frozen Friday close, not a live quote. Range = (high − low) ÷ median. Yahoo gives no date per target, and targets lag price moves: after a fall, many closes sit below the lowest target. Positive implied upside is the usual sell-side stance, not cheapness.

      Dates. Ratings, revisions and targets are dated by when Temper Lab retrieved them, not when analysts published them. Analyst opinion is shown as published; it has not passed a Leg D test as a signal, so nothing here is ranked by it. Every column with a button sorts the table (click again to flip; n/a sinks last).
