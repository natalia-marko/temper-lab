How to read these numbers & limits

Ratings mix & Buy share: Hold is Yahoo’s label—not “Keep.” Buy share divides Strong Buy plus Buy by all five categories. 1m mix Δ is this month’s Buy share minus last month’s, in percentage points; total coverage can change between months (shown as n: prior → current), so it reflects mix drift, not a count of upgrades. A missing summary or month is shown as n/a, never counted as zero votes.

EPS 30d (0y): Current fiscal-year upward and downward EPS estimate revisions over the last 30 days from the matching weekly Research snapshot. Price targets & Implied upside: Target median, low, high and implied upside use the frozen Friday close, not a live quote. Range is (high − low) / median. Positive implied upside is the usual sell-side stance, not cheapness or a Temper Lab rank. Every column header and category label sorts the table (high–low first, click again to flip; ties break by Buy share, then ticker; n/a sinks last).

Analyst conviction
