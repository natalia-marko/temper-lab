How to read this

Purpose. From the SEC transaction code, the filing's 10b5-1 box and footnotes attached to each trade. Code P or S can be open-market or private; a trade is called private only when its footnote says so.

Plan. A trade counts as under a 10b5-1 plan when its own footnote says so, or the filing's plan box is checked. The adoption date is shown when the footnote states it.

Transfers. A same-day purchase and sale of the same shares at the same price, such as a sale to one's own family trust, is one holding moving, not market buying or selling. It is counted once.

Counted once. Joint filers and amendments that restate their original. Amendments that change only part of a filing, and identical trades by unrelated filers, stay out of totals until checked.

Holding change. One disclosed account within one filing, not total wealth; unknown when balances do not reconcile.

Coverage. Form 4 and Form 5 for every issuer in the security mapping, over the last 21 trading days by New York filing day. Form 144, Schedule 13D/13G, plan adoptions in 10-Q/10-K and 13F come next.
