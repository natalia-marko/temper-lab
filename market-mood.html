What these charts measure

Returns use adjusted closes over 63 or 252 trading sessions. Each stock has equal weight in participation, the distribution and sector medians; index funds have their own weights. The median is the middle stock return. Eligible is the liquid universe with both return windows. On lists is the union of this week's Hot tape, Growth and Cheap names (rule-selected, so it can look stronger or weaker than the full eligible set). Stocks missing either return window are excluded from both windows. Today's lists are selected partly using these past returns: this describes their current composition, not how a previously selected portfolio performed.

Prices: saved Yahoo Finance captures (SPY · QQQ). Volatility: Cboe VIX history. VIX measures expected 30-day S&P 500 volatility in index points. The Shortlist line counts this week's Shortlist (names on two or more of Hot tape, Growth and Cheap); List check describes the lists before the month-end decision. Earnings dates: Yahoo Finance's earnings calendar (company pages fill gaps), refreshed Tuesday and Friday; a date can move until the company confirms it. For planning, not a signal: our test found no price drift after results. This page describes saved observations, not a forecast.
