What these charts measure

Returns use adjusted closes over 63 or 252 trading sessions. Each stock has equal weight in participation, the distribution and sector medians; index funds have their own weights. The median is the middle stock return. The comparison uses the current eligible universe, not historical market membership. Stocks missing either return window are excluded from both windows.

Prices: saved Yahoo Finance captures (SPY · QQQ). Volatility: Cboe VIX history. VIX measures expected 30-day S&P 500 volatility in index points.
