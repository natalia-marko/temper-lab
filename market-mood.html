What these charts measure

Returns use adjusted closes over 63 or 252 trading sessions; the window applies to both tabs. Each stock has equal weight in participation, the spread and sector medians; index funds have their own weights. The median is the middle stock return. Eligible is the liquid universe with both return windows. On lists is the union of this week's Hot tape, Growth and Cheap names. Sectors on both tabs use the shared industry map plus reviewed corrections; the Overview counts stocks with both return windows, List check all eligible names. Today's lists are selected partly using these past returns: the comparison describes their current composition, not how a previously selected portfolio performed.

Prices: saved Yahoo Finance captures (SPY · QQQ). Volatility: Cboe VIX history. VIX measures expected 30-day S&P 500 volatility in index points. The Shortlist counts names on two or more of Hot tape, Growth and Cheap with positive equity. Earnings dates: Yahoo Finance's earnings calendar (company pages fill gaps), refreshed Tuesday and Friday, one calendar for both tabs; List check's dates between the freeze and the refresh come from the calendar saved at the freeze. A date can move until the company confirms it. For planning, not a signal: our test found no price drift after results. This page describes saved observations, not a forecast.
