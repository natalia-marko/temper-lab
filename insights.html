How to read this research

Purchases and sales are separate totals of included Form 4 common or ordinary share transactions. Grants, exercises and tax withholding are excluded. Ambiguous amendments, ownership overlaps and security classes stay out of totals and appear for review. Partial totals are labelled.

Holdings percentages describe one disclosed account within one filing. Multiple lots are reconciled before calculating a percentage. An account starting from zero is shown separately; this is not a measure of personal wealth. A blank or unchecked plan box does not prove discretion.

The cluster view requires at least two reporting-owner groups within ten exchange sessions. Joint filers count once. This threshold is a descriptive filter, not a tested investment rule. Historical routine-versus-unusual behavior is not yet established.

Coverage is the current frozen eligible US stock universe and Form 4/4-A filings listed in its SEC issuer submissions. This is not all US securities or a historical backtest. Schedule 13D/13G catalysts and Form 144 intent are not included in these purchase/sale totals.
