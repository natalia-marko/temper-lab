How the book is built

One company, one current suggestion. The book uses the Research Shortlist for the selected Friday. July stays until a later earnings release replaces it. A newly added Shortlist name with an older release is not a new filing. Source gaps are shown explicitly.

Two answers. Raised and Cut describe guidance versus the company’s own previous guidance, not against what analysts expected. Growth describes the operating setup; it does not establish a guidance raise. Both means raised guidance and a growth setup. Maintained means repeated guidance. No comparison includes new guidance without a comparable baseline, withheld guidance, and unclear evidence.

Confidence is the model’s own number. It is not a calibrated probability of correctness or future returns. Both uses the lower of the two confidence values. A value below 0.50 stays in Other suggestions; this is a display rule. A correction does not inherit the original answer’s confidence. Non-comparable periods stay in Other suggestions even when the operating answer describes growth.

Evidence first. Delta lines are verbatim extracts, not generated explanations. The two guidance passages are shown together, with separate operating evidence and direct SEC links. Any later extraction or guidance correction is labeled separately from the input Jev originally read. Automatic checks cover selected formats; they do not certify every interpretation.

Returns are realized context. Each figure is the stock’s adjusted return minus QQQ’s adjusted return in percentage points. The 3-session reaction is around the first session after the filing. The later window runs from the next session’s close for 21 session intervals. Only complete windows are shown. Prices, momentum and the arithmetic checks do not order the book.

Arithmetic checks are context. Diluted share growth at most 10%, a positive operating or free-cash-flow-after-stock-pay margin, and a drawdown below 60%. Missing data is shown. These checks do not certify the guidance read or remove a company from the book.
