How a test works: 1. Written down → 2. Recorded → 3. Judged

1Written down

The rule, what it is compared with and what would count as a win are saved with a date and a fingerprint, before any new data arrives.

2Recorded

Each week’s top 10 is saved before the market opens, bought on paper at the next open, and held for 6 and 12 months.

3Judged

Only finished batches count, against QQQ and SPY. A challenger replaces a list only by its written rule, never by a hunch.
